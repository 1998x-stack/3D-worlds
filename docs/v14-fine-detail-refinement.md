## Engine and portable-source compatibility

Three portable primitives were added to the existing `buildRecipe` engine: rounded extrusion (`rounded-box`), six-sided prism (`hex`) and partial torus (`arc`). No extra JavaScript package is needed beyond Three.js; the engine uses only Three.js core geometries, so the Gallery can still bundle a copyable standalone source snippet.

Nine conservative, glTF-compatible surface treatments are available: matte (legacy default), anodized, ceramic, metal, rubber, fabric, wood, glass and glow. GLB export and thumbnail rendering should be regenerated as part of CI; imagery is never claimed to be committed binary source.

## Honest interpretation of improvement

The changes create more legible visual form and certain physically open *representations* (soap drainage slats and shade brick apertures). They are still mesh-primitive scene objects; the existing scale-normalization scheme is **not** dimensionally constrained engineering CAD. Glass is a rendering material and does not form a manufacturable glass part. Fine labels and microtextures may be under slicer resolution after mm scaling. Mechanisms are aesthetic and are not tested to move or carry load.
